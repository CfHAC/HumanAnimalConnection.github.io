Why?

We were youths once, and we know how important it is to have space to try, grow, and be heard. Through these programmes, we hope every young person can experience the connection with animals and the sense of care it brings.

These programmes also aim to give young people the chance to shape ideas, build confidence, and make a difference through running animal-assisted activities.

How?

We run sessions with animals that bring calm and connection, hold circles that spark friendships, and guide train-the-trainer programmes where youths run their own projects - for youth, by youth, and one day - for all, by youth.

Contact Us

Reach out to us to find out more about our youth programmes and how they can support your team or organisation. Together, we can do good for our communities by giving youth the chance to grow alongside animals and each other.

Hall/Faculty Visits

Our faculty visits bring CHAC to schools, hospitals, and community centers to provide a unique and heartwarming experience for participants of all ages and abilities. These visits offer a refreshing break from routine and a chance for individuals to experience the joy and healing power that animals bring into our lives.

During these visits, we bring in our animals to offer joy, comfort, and companionship through casual interactions. With no structured format, these visits allow individuals to engage and bond with our animals in a relaxed and informal setting.

Duration: 45 minutes (guinea pigs, rabbits, cats, dogs) to 90 minutes (dogs only).

Train-the-trainer

Our "Train-the-trainer" program equips youths to run sessions for their peers and other groups, including individuals with diverse abilities and seniors. The training covers both theory and practical application, preparing youth leaders to design and facilitate safe, meaningful activities with animals.

Target Group: Student leaders, Student-led groups, Service learning or Volunteer Clubs.

Youths take part in a training session where they learn the basics of animal-assisted activities. They practice how to plan and lead safe, meaningful sessions for peers, individuals with diverse abilities, and seniors. With both theory and hands-on practice, they gain the skills to confidently run activities in their own groups or communities.

Contact: hello@chacsg.com

Design to Deliver

Our Design to Deliver program is created for youths. We either design customised sessions based on your goals, or guide students to design their own program, which we then help bring to life. This approach gives youth ownership of the learning while ensuring a meaningful and engaging experience.

How it Works: First, we meet with your team to understand your objectives. Based on this, our facilitators create a customised plan, or support students in designing one themselves. Once the plan is ready, we deliver the sessions, combining both structured guidance and hands-on activities with animals.

Duration: Sessions run 45 minutes when working with cats, guinea pigs, or rabbits, and up to 1.5 hours with dogs. Programs can also be extended into a series of workshops.

Target Group: Youths, including school and community groups.

Contact: hello@chacsg.com
