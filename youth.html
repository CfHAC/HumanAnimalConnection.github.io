Why?

We were youths once, and we know how important it is to have space to try, grow, and be heard. Through these programmes, we hope every young person can experience the connection with animals and the sense of care it brings.

These programmes also aim to give young people the chance to shape ideas, build confidence, and make a difference through running animal-assisted activities.

How?

We run sessions with animals that bring calm and connection, hold circles that spark friendships, and guide train-the-trainer programmes where youths run their own projects - for youth, by youth, and one day- for all, by youth.

Contact Us

Reach out to us to find out more about our youth programmes and how they can support your team or organisation. Together, we can do good for our communities by giving youth the chance to grow alongside animals and each other.

Hall/Faculty Visits

Our faculty visits bring CHAC to schools, hospitals, and community centers to provide a unique and heartwarming experience for participants of all ages and abilities. These visits offer a refreshing break from routine and a chance for individuals to experience the joy and healing power that animals bring into our lives.

During these visits, we bring in our animals to offer joy, comfort, and companionship through casual interactions. With no structured format, these visits allow individuals to engage and bond with our animals in a relaxed and informal setting.

How it Works

In our Informal AAA Institutional Visits with Animals, we create an environment of relaxation and spontaneity. Our well-trained animals, accompanied by experienced handlers, engage in casual interactions with participants.

These visits have no structured format, allowing individuals to freely connect with our animals at their own pace. Participants can enjoy petting, cuddling, and spending quality time with the animals, promoting a sense of joy and emotional well-being.

Duration

The sessions span from 45 minutes (guinea pigs, rabbits, cats, dogs) to 90 minutes (dogs only).

Price

We understand the value of these heartwarming interactions, and we strive to make them accessible to as many institutions as possible.

For detailed pricing information and to discuss your specific needs, please reach out to us at hello@chacsg.com. We are dedicated to fostering meaningful connections and spreading happiness through our informal animal-assisted activities, and we look forward to making a difference in your community.

Contact

To schedule a memorable and heartwarming Hall/Faculty Visit(s), please contact us at hello@chacsg.com. We look forward to spreading happiness and fostering human-animal connections together!

Train-the-trainer

Our "Train-the-trainer" program equips youths to run sessions for their peers and other groups, including individuals with diverse abilities and seniors. The training covers both theory and practical application, preparing youth leaders to design and facilitate safe, meaningful activities with animals.

Who is the Target Group?

Student leaders, Student led groups, Service learning or Volunteer Clubs!

How It Works:

Youths take part in a training session where they learn the basics of animal-assisted activities. They practice how to plan and lead safe, meaningful sessions for peers, individuals with diverse abilities, and seniors. With both theory and hands-on practice, they gain the skills to confidently run activities in their own groups or communities.

Price:

At Centre for Human-Animal Connection, we believe in making animal-assisted interactions accessible to all. We offer flexible pricing options for our "Train-the-trainer" program, tailored to accommodate different budgets and settings.

For detailed information about our pricing structure and package options, please get in touch with us at hello@chacsg.com. We'll be more than happy to assist you in planning a memorable and transformative experience with our beloved animal friends.

Join Us:

Join us in creating beautiful memories and building meaningful connections through equiping! To know more about our program and pricing structure, please contact us at hello@chacsg.com.

Design to Deliver

Our Design to Deliver program is created for youths. We either design customised sessions based on your goals, or guide students to design their own program, which we then help bring to life. This approach gives youth ownership of the learning while ensuring a meaningful and engaging experience.

How it Works

First, we meet with your team to understand your objectives. Based on this, our facilitators create a customised plan, or support students in designing one themselves. Once the plan is ready, we deliver the sessions, combining both structured guidance and hands-on activities with animals.

Duration

Sessions run 45 minutes when working with cats, guinea pigs, or rabbits, and up to 1.5 hours with dogs. Programs can also be extended into a series of workshops.

Target Group

This program is designed for youths, including school and community groups.

Contact

To explore a customised Design to Deliver program for your group, reach us at hello@chacsg.com.
