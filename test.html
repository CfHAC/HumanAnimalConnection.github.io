Hall/Faculty Visits

We bring animals into halls or faculties for informal, joyful interactions.

How it Works

Animals engage casually with participants, creating a calm and uplifting space.

Duration

45 mins with small animals, up to 1.5 hours with dogs.

Train-the-trainer

Youths are trained to run activities for peers, seniors, and groups with diverse abilities.

Target Group

Student leaders, service learning groups, and volunteer clubs.

How it Works

We cover theory and hands-on practice so youths can confidently lead safe, meaningful sessions.

Design to Deliver

We co-create programs with youth, or support them to design their own activities with animals.

How it Works

We align with your goals, design or guide youths to design sessions, and deliver them together.

Duration

45 mins with small animals, 1.5 hours with dogs, or extended series.
